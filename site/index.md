# EyeSense

**Your world, described.**

EyeSense is an AI visual assistance app for blind and low-vision users. Point your phone's camera at something and it tells you what is there — out loud, designed to be used entirely without sight.

## What it does

- **Read Text** recognises printed text and reads it aloud. It runs **entirely on your device** and needs no internet connection at all.
- **Describe Scene** takes one photograph and describes what is in front of you.
- **Ask a Question** lets you ask something specific about what the camera can see.

Describe Scene and Ask a Question send a photograph off your device, so they only work after you explicitly turn on cloud processing. Until you do, nothing leaves your phone.

## What it is not

EyeSense is **not a mobility aid and not a medical device.** It will not tell you whether it is safe to cross a road, whether a path is clear, or how far away something is, and it refuses those questions on purpose. It describes one still photograph, and it can be wrong.

Use it alongside your cane, guide dog, training and judgement — never instead of them. The [Terms of Use](/terms) explain this in full, and it is worth reading section 2.

## Privacy, briefly

There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing you capture is used to train AI models. Read Text never uses the network.

The [Privacy Policy](/privacy) sets out exactly what happens to each photograph, what our service sees when you use a cloud feature, and how long anything is kept.
